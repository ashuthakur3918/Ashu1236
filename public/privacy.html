FRONTIER WORLD

Privacy,
plainly stated.

Last updated: 30 September 2026. This page describes the information Frontier receives through the current site features and how it is used.

TRIP + BUSINESS ENQUIRIES

What you provide

Name, email, phone, dates, destination, group size, interests, budget, starting location, business details, website and any message you submit through a form.

NEWSLETTER

Your email address

If you subscribe to the Frontier Weekender, Frontier receives the email address and submission details needed to manage the subscription and send the newsletter.

MY FRONTIER

Account and saved travel data

Signed-in features can store saved places and trip plans. Account access uses the site's passwordless email-code flow.

PLANNING TOOLS

Travel requests

Questions submitted through Frontier planning tools are processed to provide the requested travel guidance. Do not submit passwords, payment details, identity-document numbers or other sensitive information.

SITE USAGE

Basic analytics

The site records page views, searches and selected product interactions for site analytics and troubleshooting. Frontier does not present these analytics as a travel profile or sell them as a recommendation service.

COOKIES + STORAGE

Essential site functions

Signed-in features may use cookies or browser storage managed by the hosting/authentication platform. Frontier also uses browser storage for accessibility preferences where supported. See the cookie notice for the current site-level approach.

WHY WE USE INFORMATION

Operate the site.
Respond to you.

Provide requested features

We use submitted information to operate trip enquiries, business enquiries, newsletter delivery, saved places and trip plans.

Improve reliability

Basic interaction data can help identify broken flows, understand which pages are useful and improve the product.

Keep travel information honest

Frontier may retain editorial and verification notes so that current claims can be reviewed and updated.

IMPORTANT

Travel information
is not a guarantee.

Prices, availability, permits, weather, road conditions, safety notices, entry rules and other time-sensitive facts can change. Use official government, local authority and provider sources before making decisions.

For privacy questions, use the Frontier enquiry form.
