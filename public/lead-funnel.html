QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Not sure what to enter?

Do I need exact dates?

No. The form includes flexibility options, so you can submit an approximate period or say that dates are not decided yet.

What if I do not know the destination?

Use “not sure yet” in the destination field and explain the kind of trip you want in the requirements box.

Is this a booking?

No. This is an enquiry. Prices, availability, permits and current travel conditions should be verified before travel.

Can groups and businesses use it?

Yes. The funnel captures different enquiry types and adds company or agency context when relevant.

How much detail should I provide?

Enough to explain what matters. The most useful context is usually destination, dates or flexibility, group size, budget and specific requirements.
